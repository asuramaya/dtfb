Description
This 2022 Ford Escape SE in Black delivers practical daily driving with a refined approach to compact SUV ownership.

– 1.5L EcoBoost® engine with 8-speed automatic transmission
– SE Sport Appearance Package with 19″ machined-face aluminum wheels
– Convenience Package including dual-zone climate control and 8-way power driver seat
– Power liftgate for convenient cargo access
– SYNC® 3 communications and entertainment system with SiriusXM
– Halogen projector headlamps with LED signature and configurable daytime running lamps
– Exterior rear parking camera
– Fold-down rear center armrest with cupholders
– Electronic stability control and traction control
– Four-wheel independent suspension
– Split-folding rear seat for flexible cargo configuration
– FordPass Connect integration
– SYNC® 3 911 Assist emergency communication system

The powertrain balances efficiency with capability, delivering 28 city and 34 highway miles per gallon. This configuration suits drivers who value both responsiveness in urban environments and composed highway performance without compromise.

The Sport Appearance Package elevates the visual presence with its distinctive 19-inch wheels and gloss black grille treatment, while the black bumpers and mirror caps reinforce a cohesive design aesthetic. The interior reflects a driver-focused approach with the 8-way power driver seat offering multiple adjustment options, including lumbar support and recline functionality.

Climate management through dual-zone electronic automatic temperature control ensures all occupants maintain their preferred comfort levels. The power liftgate removes a daily friction point, making loading and unloading intuitive. SYNC® 3 integrates smartphone connectivity and entertainment seamlessly, while the rear parking camera provides confidence during maneuvers.

Safety systems include four-wheel independent suspension for composed handling, electronic stability control, and comprehensive airbag protection. The four-wheel disc braking system with ABS delivers responsive stopping power across varied driving conditions.

This Escape SE represents straightforward, capable transportation with thoughtful comfort features and modern conveniences that support your daily routine. Visit us to experience how it fits your driving needs.
